# English store listing

Name: TableFlow

Short description: Copy web tables to Excel or export CSV locally, with notices when data may be incomplete.

The short description comes from the localized extension manifest; Partner Center reads this field from the uploaded package.

## Full description

Move web tables into your spreadsheet with fewer manual steps. Click TableFlow to discover tables on the current page, check their preview and data scope, then copy or export the selected table.

Useful for order lists, product records, public data tables, and everyday office work. Processing stays in your browser and requires no account or server.

- Discover and select tables, with a preview before export.
- Copy rows and columns to Excel, or create UTF-8 CSV with a BOM for compatible spreadsheet applications.
- Read standard HTML tables and common web data grids. Actual support depends on each page's structure.
- See current-page and incomplete-data warnings. Risky exports require your confirmation of the available scope.
- Optionally enable table copy icons for individual websites, with permission requested for that website.
- Simplified Chinese and English interface.

## Privacy

TableFlow reads the table structure and text on pages you choose to use. It processes that content locally and does not upload it to the publisher, AI, or third parties. There is no advertising tracking, analytics, or telemetry. Only preferences such as language, onboarding status, and enabled website rules are saved locally.

Copying writes data to your system clipboard; exporting creates local files. Diagnostic reports are copied or downloaded only at your request. Review them before sharing.

## Scope and limitations

Some pages cannot be read completely. Pagination exports the current page and does not automatically turn pages. Inaccessible frames, closed components, unloaded data, and horizontally virtualized columns may be unavailable. Collecting more virtual rows requires your action and may still produce incomplete results. “No missing data detected” describes evidence from the current page structure rather than an absolute guarantee. Check the preview and warnings before exporting.

## Help

Let the page finish loading, then scan again. If a problem remains, use Help to generate a local diagnostic report that excludes business cell content.

For support, privacy questions, or compatibility reports, contact the maintainer through [TableFlow GitHub Issues](https://github.com/ASOAT/tableflow/issues). Issues are public. Do not post sensitive table content, personal information, passwords, tokens, cookies, or complete business datasets. Review diagnostic reports and screenshots before sharing them.
